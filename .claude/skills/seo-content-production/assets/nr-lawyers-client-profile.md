## Voice Pillars

| Pillar | What it means | Example | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authoritative | Every claim earns its place — grounded in a specific, verifiable credential, not a vague superlative | "Joseph Neuberger holds the Law Society of Ontario Certified Specialist designation — held by fewer than 2% of Ontario criminal lawyers." | Vague superlatives, unsubstantiated claims, passive constructions |
| Direct | Someone reading this is in crisis and has no patience for a slow build-up | "If you've been charged with sexual assault in Ontario, the decisions you make in the next 24 hours matter." | Long wind-ups, buried calls to action |
| Sensitive | Never sensationalizes, never implies guilt, never adds to the reader's shame | "These charges carry serious personal consequences. We handle every case with the discretion and urgency it deserves." | Fear-based language, sensational phrasing, anything implying guilt |
| Credible | Claims grounded in documented outcomes, pointing to the Recent Successes page rather than making abstract claims | "See our Recent Successes — a published record of real case outcomes, updated regularly." | Outcome guarantees, unqualified superlatives |
| Accessible | Legal expertise explained in plain language a frightened non-lawyer can act on immediately | "Being charged doesn't mean being convicted. Here's what a sexual assault defence actually looks like." | Dense legal terminology without explanation |

## Core Messages

1. **Specialization is the standard, not a marketing angle.** A lawyer who has spent 30 years on sexual assault and domestic assault specifically is not interchangeable with a general criminal lawyer — this is a factual claim about depth of practice, not a boast.
2. **Results are documented, not claimed.** The firm publishes case outcomes; this is unusual in the market and should be pointed to constantly rather than replaced with abstract claims of success.
3. **Response speed matters and is proven.** 24/7 intake, same-day lawyer contact for serious charges, and an 18% PPC conversion rate that shows the intake process genuinely works.
4. **Discretion is non-negotiable.** Solicitor-client privilege from the first call; no public commentary on active cases.
5. **Ontario-wide, not Toronto-only.** Serious charges bring clients who are willing to travel for the right specialist; every Ontario courthouse is in scope.
