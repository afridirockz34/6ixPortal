## Shared rules across all templates

**Never pad to hit a word count.** Every section earns its place by delivering real information. Thin content with accurate, specific detail outperforms padded content with the same word count — this is true both for readers and for how search engines evaluate the page.

**One idea per paragraph, 2-4 sentences, white space between.** Nobody reads dense paragraphs on a phone. Break any sentence over ~25 words into two.

**Ground every specific claim in the client profile.** A statistic, a credential, a case outcome, a named court — if it's not in the profile, don't write it. Flag the gap instead.

**Headings are questions or topics a reader is actually asking**, not generic labels. "What Happens After You're Charged" beats "Overview." This also happens to be what makes a heading eligible to be pulled into a featured snippet — the answer should appear in the first 40-60 words after the heading, then expand.

**FAQ sections are for readers, not for schema.** Write them because real people have these questions and a good page answers them. FAQPage structured data no longer produces a rich result in Google Search (confirmed as of mid-2026) — keep writing FAQ content because it's useful, not because of a rich-result payoff that doesn't exist anymore. Check `ymyl-legal-pack.md` or search current documentation before assuming any schema type has a visible payoff.

**Meta title** under 60 characters, primary keyword near the front, brand name at the end. **Meta description** under 160 characters, keyword included naturally, ends with a direct call to action.

**No keyword density target.** Use the keyword and its natural variations where the content genuinely calls for them — in the H1, early in the intro, in a couple of subheadings, in the conclusion. Don't count percentages; write for the reader and the keyword coverage takes care of itself for anyone actually answering the query thoroughly.

## Template 1: Blog Post

**Defaults:** 1,200-2,000 words (1,500 target). Minimum 3 internal links (1-2 to relevant service/product pages, 1 to a related post or the contact/conversion page). Minimum 5 FAQ questions.

**Meta block** (fill before writing): SEO title, meta description, primary keyword, secondary keyword, target long-tail phrase, author (with their credential, if the profile has one), publish date, category/cluster.

**Introduction** (150-250 words) — Open with the reader's actual situation, not a dictionary definition. State what's at stake for them. Promise what this piece explains. Get the primary keyword in naturally within the first 100 words.

**Body sections** (each roughly 250-350 words, adapt the number and titles to the topic):
- Understanding the situation / the underlying facts, in plain language with any necessary technical terms defined on first use
- What happens next / the process, structured as numbered steps if there's a sequence — this structure is what makes a section eligible for a featured snippet
- How to protect yourself / what to do about it, specific to this topic, grounded in the client's actual expertise from the profile — never phrased as a guaranteed outcome
- Why this client specifically is positioned to help, drawing on the profile's credentials and differentiators, with a link to the relevant service or about page

**FAQ section** — minimum 5 questions, each answered in 60-120 words. Include at minimum: a process/rights question, a cost or timeline question, a location-specific question if relevant, and a "what should I do next" question.

**Conclusion** (150-200 words) — Three concrete takeaways, restate what's at stake, a direct call to action with contact information. Link to one relevant service page and one related post.

**Author block** — name, credential, role, link to their bio page if one exists.

## Template 2: Location Page

**Defaults:** 1,000-1,500 words (1,200 target). Minimum 3 internal links (to the primary relevant service page, 1-2 nearby location pages, and the contact page).

**Meta title:** `[Primary Service] [City] | [Client Name]` — under 60 characters.
**Meta description:** the city-specific situation, the client's relevant credential, a call to action — under 160 characters.

**H1:** `[Primary Service] [City]` — a subheading immediately below naming the specific value proposition for this city.

**Intro** (no H2, 200-300 words) — Paragraph 1: urgency plus a specific, real local detail (a courthouse name, a neighborhood, a genuinely local reference — never a generic city mention that could be swapped for any other city). Paragraph 2: what's at stake. Paragraph 3: a soft call to action with the phone number.

**Services in [City]** (300-400 words) — Cover each relevant service with a one-paragraph summary and a link to its full service page.

**Why choose [Client] in [City]** (200-300 words) — The credentials and differentiators from the profile, made locally relevant where possible.

**What happens after [the triggering event] in [City]** (200-300 words) — A numbered process specific to how this actually works locally if the process has local variation (which court, which specific procedure) — verify this detail is real and current, don't guess at local process specifics.

**Courts/facilities serving [City]** (150-250 words, if relevant to the business type) — Name and address, and the client's actual familiarity with that specific venue if the profile supports the claim.

**FAQ — [Category] in [City]** (minimum 5 questions) — Include: a location-logistics question, a "is a [City] lawyer/provider necessary or can I use one from elsewhere" question, a response-time question, and a free-consultation-or-equivalent question.

**Serving [City] and surrounding areas** (80-120 words) — List 3-5 genuinely nearby areas, link to their location pages if they exist.

**Closing CTA** (100-150 words) — Direct urgency-appropriate call to action with contact information.

## Template 3: Service Page

**Defaults:** 1,500-2,500 words (2,000 target). Minimum 4 internal links (related services, relevant location pages, team/about page, a proof/results page).

**Required section checklist** — treat this as a build checklist, not just a description:

- [ ] H1 with the primary keyword
- [ ] A trust bar near the top: the strongest 3-4 credentials from the profile, stated as facts
- [ ] A call to action above the fold — phone number and a contact method
- [ ] Section 1: Understanding [the service/situation] (300-400 words) — what it is, in plain language, with technical terms defined on first use
- [ ] Section 2: The process (400-500 words) — numbered steps, structured for snippet eligibility
- [ ] Section 3: Why choose [Client] (250-350 words) — credentials plus documented outcomes, never phrased as a guarantee
- [ ] Section 4: Proof / results (150-200 words) — link to the client's actual case studies, results page, or portfolio; never fabricate an example
- [ ] Section 5: FAQ (500-700 words, minimum 6 questions)
- [ ] Section 6: Areas served (80-120 words, links to 2-3 location pages)
- [ ] Closing CTA block — phone, contact form, and the client's actual availability claim (only if true — verify against the profile, don't assume 24/7 or same-day unless the profile states it)
- [ ] Author attribution with credential and bio link

## Template 4: Audience Page

For a specific reader segment the client serves differently from their general audience — a distinct professional group, a specific demographic, a specific situation ("just started," "referred by a friend," "comparing options"). Not every client needs these; build one only where the profile identifies a genuinely distinct audience with distinct concerns.

**Defaults:** 1,200-1,800 words. Minimum 4 internal links.

**Meta title:** `[Audience Situation] [City/Region] | [Client Name]` — under 60 characters.
**H1:** the audience's situation stated in their own likely words, e.g. "Just Been Diagnosed?" or "New to Investing?" — not a generic label like "For Beginners."

**Intro** (no H2, 200-300 words) — Paragraph 1: their exact situation in the first two sentences. Paragraph 2: their specific fears or stakes (drawn from the profile's audience research, not invented). Paragraph 3: how this client specifically addresses exactly this, with a soft CTA.

**Challenges facing [Audience]** (250-350 words) — 4-6 challenge/response pairs specific to this audience, not generic.

**Services for [Audience]** (300-400 words) — Relevant services framed for this audience's specific concern, with internal links.

**Why [Audience] choose [Client]** (200-300 words) — Credentials specifically relevant to why this audience benefits.

**Our process for [Audience]** (200-300 words) — The client's actual process, as it applies to this audience.

**FAQ — [Audience]** (400-500 words, minimum 5-6 audience-specific questions).

**Take the first step** (100-150 words) — Empathetic, low-pressure CTA. If the client offers confidentiality or privilege protections relevant to this audience's hesitation, state it plainly and accurately.
