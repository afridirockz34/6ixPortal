## 2. Backlink Profile — High

Harbourline Dental currently has 68 referring domains, with a Domain Rating of 24 — roughly a third of the two competitors identified in Section 5 (both sit between DR 38-45). Of those 68 referring domains, 9 are DR 35+ and currently link to pages that return a 404: three to a discontinued "Emergency Services" page, four to an old blog post removed in a 2024 site redesign, and two to a staff bio page for a provider no longer with the practice.

That's real, already-earned authority sitting on dead links instead of passing value to a live page. It isn't costing anything new each day, but it also isn't helping — and it's the kind of gap that compounds: the longer those links point at dead pages, the more likely some of those 9 referring sites eventually just remove the link rather than fix it on their end.

**What this costs:** roughly a fifth of the site's strongest inbound authority is currently pointed at nothing.

**What addressing it involves:** a redirect-mapping pass matching each broken-link target to the right live page, plus a short outreach pass on the three highest-value ones where a direct correction (not just a redirect) is worth asking for. This is part of the technical SEO engagement, not a standalone fix.

---

Note what this section does and doesn't do: it names an exact number, ties it to a specific real-sounding cause (the redesign, the departed staff member), and quantifies the impact in plain terms — but it stops at "a redirect-mapping pass... is part of the technical SEO engagement," not a walkthrough of how to build the redirect map.
