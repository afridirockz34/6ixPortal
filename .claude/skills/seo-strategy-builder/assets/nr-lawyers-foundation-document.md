## Unique Value Proposition (3 pillars, each grounded in a specific fact)

1. **30 years of deep specialization, not general practice.** A lawyer who has spent three decades specifically on sexual assault and domestic assault defence is a different value proposition than a general criminal lawyer — this is a factual claim about depth of practice.
2. **Documented, not claimed, results.** A "Recent Successes" page updated weekly with real, verifiable case outcomes — an unusually consistent transparency practice in this market.
3. **Proven intake quality.** An 18% PPC conversion rate against an ~8% industry average — evidence that the first-contact experience genuinely works, not just an assumption.

## Target Audiences

1. The accused, directly — high-net-worth individuals, ages ~30-60, concerned about time sensitivity, anonymity/safety, and proven success rate.
2. Family members calling on someone's behalf — spouses, parents, adult children, concerns filtered through worry for someone else.
3. Referred or repeat clients — professionals, doctors, executives, high lifetime value, high discretion requirement.
